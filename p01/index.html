Our manifesto

We believe music deserves to move. For too long, songs have sat perfectly still inside glass rectangles, waiting to be scrolled past. No more. Every song you play on P-01 will physically rotate, slowly, at roughly 8.6 revolutions per minute. Is it necessary? No. Did anyone ask for it? Also no. But somebody had to stand up for spinning. That somebody is a 19-year-old in Hobart with a soldering iron.

Our journey.

Every pivot so far, from a dream to circuit boards in a JLCPCB cart.

  1. rev 0.1

    Day one

    The founder had a vision: a TP-7-inspired slab with an iPod-style wheel, Bluetooth and downloads. He told nobody, because of stealth mode.

  2. rev 0.1.1

    Pivot #1: thinner

    Squarer corners and a 12 mm body. We listened to our users (we asked ourselves).

  3. rev 0.2

    Deep tech era

    ESP32-WROVER-E chosen for Bluetooth audio, Wi-Fi sync and 8 MB of PSRAM. First circuit boards: a 4-layer main board and a 2-layer controls board.

  4. rev 0.2.1

    Our first crisis

    The power switch was facing into the case. Twice. We fixed it and wrote a heartfelt post about failure.

  5. rev 0.3

    Product-market spin

    A 6 mm gear motor turns the wheel through a rubber roller. A diametric magnet and an AS5600 read the angle. The Rotational Deficit was born.

  6. rev 0.3.1

    Going to manufacturing

    The screen got an even 7 mm border on the top and sides. The buttons joined the JLCPCB assembly files. We are, technically, a hardware company now.

Questions investors keep asking.

Nobody has asked these. We wrote them ourselves.

Is it on the blockchain?

No. It's on a microSD card.

Does it have AI?

It has a magnet. The magnet is extremely smart about which way it's pointing.

Why does the wheel spin?

To solve the Rotational Deficit™. Also because it looks cool, which is the real reason.

Can I stream on it?

No. It syncs albums from your own music server over Wi-Fi, then plays them offline. We call this "owning things".

When does it ship?

The circuit boards are in a JLCPCB cart right now. That's basically shipping.

P-01 Pocket Player

Built around an ESP32-WROVER-E, with a small motor that spins the wheel while music plays. Drag around the wheel to scroll, tap it to select, and use the buttons as normal. Slide it apart to see every part inside, in the order it stacks.

Drag to turn · drag around the wheel to scroll · tap the wheel to select

Spec sheet

Size
66 × 112 × 12 mm
Brain
ESP32-WROVER-E module: dual-core 240 MHz, 8 MB PSRAM, Wi-Fi and Bluetooth Classic
Prototype
Build it first on a LilyGO T8 dev board (same module, with microSD and charging built in), then move to a custom main board like the one in this model
Storage
microSD card inside, read in fast 4-bit mode and filled over Wi-Fi
Screen
2.0" IPS, 320 × 240, ST7789 driver over SPI
Wheel
46 mm wheel that spins while music plays. A 6 mm gear motor turns it through a rubber roller, which slips when you grab the wheel, so you can scrub by hand. Press the wheel to select.
Sensor
6 × 2.5 mm diametric magnet in the wheel hub, read through the controls board by an AS5600
Controls
Menu, back, play/pause, next, press-to-select wheel, volume rocker, power slider
Audio
Bluetooth (A2DP) to headphones, or wired 3.5 mm through a PCM5102A DAC
Downloads
Syncs from a Navidrome server on your home PC while paused or charging, because Wi-Fi and Bluetooth share one radio
Power
About 1000 mAh flat LiPo (4.5 × 58 × 39 mm, shortened to make room for the motor), USB-C charging, 3.3 V regulator
Battery life
Roughly 6 h with the wheel spinning, 8 h with spinning switched off (Bluetooth, estimates)
Shell
Printed PETG front plate, frame and back cover, held by 4 × M2 screws

WireThin wire (about 30AWG) for the 8 servo signals and the screen. Thicker wire (about 22AWG) for 5V, GND and the battery.
Header rowsTop row = servo signal. Middle row = 5V. Bottom row = GND. This matches the servo plug: orange, red, brown.
S2 Mini pinsGo by the numbers printed on your S2 Mini. The drawing has its USB-C plug at the bottom.
How to use thisTap a step to light up its wires. Tick each wire as you solder it. Your ticks are saved in this browser.

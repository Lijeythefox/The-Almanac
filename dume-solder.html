Soldering order, polarity and first power-upDUM-E Soldering Guide

OrderShortest parts to tallest. Each step is one height, so the board rests flat on its parts while you solder.
Square pad ruleElectrolytic cap: long leg (+). LED and diode: cathode (short leg / band). Modules and terminals: pin 1.
Iron330–350 °C for small joints. ~380 °C with a chisel tip for terminals, C13 and anything on a big ground area.
How to use thisTap a step to light up its parts on the board. Tick parts as you go. Your ticks are saved in this browser.
